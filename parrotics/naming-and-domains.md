# Naming and domains

## Why "Parrotics"

**Parrot + robotics.** It sounds like a real word, it's easy to say and spell, and it carries meaning:

- Parrots learn by **imitation**, the same idea as imitation learning in robotics.
- Parrots **talk**, so it's a natural home for robots with voices.
- Colourful, friendly, intelligent, and instantly recognisable.

It was found by checking robotics wordplay names. Words ending in **-ot** plus **-ics** sound like "-otics" (parrot → Parrotics).

## Other robotics names considered (free on 2026-10-24)

Kept as backups or ideas for sub-brands:

- **Foxtrotics:** foxtrot + robotics, for walking or dancing robots
- **Sproutics:** sprout + robotics, nature and growth
- **Mascotics:** mascot + robotics, for character robots
- **Teapotics:** teapot + robotics, small home robots
- **Roboetics:** robot + poetics, expressive and artistic
- **Chariotics:** chariot + robotics, mobility
- **Moonshotics:** moonshot + robotics, research and deep-tech

**Avoid:** Waddlebotics and Quackbotics (too close to Microduck), Hobbitics (Tolkien trademark).

## Inspiration

Pollen Robotics (Bordeaux, owned by Hugging Face): **Microduck**, a 25 cm bipedal duck robot announced in August 2026 at $399 with open-source software, and **Reachy Mini**, focused on interaction.

Parrotics should stay clearly distinct from them in name, animal and focus.
